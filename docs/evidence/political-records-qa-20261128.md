# Political records ingestion and notebook QA — 2026-11-28

**Status: PARTIAL PASS / REVIEW REQUIRED.** This validates a pipeline over ten public National Archives and Records Administration (NARA) case letters. They are administrative letters about alleged or confirmed records incidents, not the underlying 7,000-email Clinton corpus and not complete email or messaging exports. NARA index status is not a criminal finding.

## Scope and provenance

- Ten official NARA PDFs, 21 pages total; acquisition inventory and per-source SHA-256 are in [case acquisition evidence](political-email-messaging-case-acquisition-20261128.md). Source PDFs and full extracted/normalized text remain outside Git.

- Poppler 26.11.0 extracted embedded PDF text. The accepted TRANS `main` implementation (`f59e9578e26540a87610e82009e5b877835b6632`) normalized page candidates. This was **not OCR**. All 21 pages remain non-final and review-required; mean heuristic confidence was 0.5452 (uncalibrated).
- Candidate text SHA-256: `725e1d1f3cd281c5c783fd737fb71a6beadc08bf4368db2525d7b1753c21e501`. Full candidate text is deliberately not stored in Git.

## Arch Notebook handoff

A disposable QA case was built and passed through the installed Rust CLI's `--attach-transcription-candidates` command for all ten PDFs. All ten attachments passed source filename/size/hash verification. Notebook reported `raw_transcription_imported=false`, `review_required=true`, and `relationships_created=0` for every attachment. The case and candidate text remain local; the portable evidence is the default-redacted packet below.

The exported packet describes ten isolated source-document entities and zero relationship records. It therefore does not assert that people or organizations contacted each other. `finding.md` and `network.svg` were generated by Arch Notebook with names, IDs, relationship labels and evidence references disabled.

## Qwen candidate checks

Qwen 3.5 9B produced completed responses for ten inputs, but one response (UD-2024-0079) was truncated and failed JSON parsing. Of the remaining nine:

- Five passed JSON structure, required review flag, internal evidence/entity reference checks, and source quote matching (exact text or normalized whitespace/typography).
- Four failed source quote matching. These candidates are held out of the presentation data.
- Quote matching checks string traceability only; it does not establish semantic support, factual correctness, attribution, or whether a suggested relationship is warranted. Even the five passing items remain machine-generated, review-required candidates and were **not imported as notebook relationships**.
- A subsequent inference rerun could not connect to the local Ollama endpoint. Existing completed responses were used; rerun status is BLOCKED.

The five eligible candidate records and per-case outcomes are in `political-records-qa/artifacts/`. Email addresses found in candidate output were redacted. Source contact data and phone/street addresses are not published as part of this presentation. The source PDF's contact information may still appear in its full local text, which is excluded.
